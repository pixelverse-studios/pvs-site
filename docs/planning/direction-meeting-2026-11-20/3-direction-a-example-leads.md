## Lead 1: Shine Line Mobile Detailing (tools-only)

**Who:** Marco runs a mobile car detailing business in Paramus with one helper. He's on a paid Wix plan for 2 more years and likes how his site looks.

**How he found us:** a Google ad for "online booking for detailers." He filled out the form from the home services landing page.

**What he says on the call:** "I'm under a car all day. By the time I call people back, they've booked someone else. And I've double-booked twice this month."

**What we recommend:** the tools-only plan, no rebuild.

- **Online booking:** he uses free Calendly today, which only allows the Embed level. We suggest a paid Cal.com or Calendly plan (his cost) so bookings reach our lead alerts and reports (Connected level).
- **Missed-call text-back:** every missed call gets a text with his booking link within a minute.
- Both tools go on his Wix site with embed code; his paid Wix plan allows it.

**Cost:** $700 setup (2 integrated tools × $350) + $198/month (2 tools × $99). Year one, with deposits added in month 3: about $4,416.

**Spread option:** not offered. The $700 setup is small enough to pay upfront.

**How it plays out:**

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Week 1–2 | Tools set up on his Wix site; first monthly report starts |
| Month 3 | Report shows no-shows; we add deposits through Stripe (+$350 setup, +$99/month) |
| Month 6 | He refers another detailer (free month for him) |
| Month 24 | Wix term ends; we pitch a Launch plan with the tools already in place |

**What it tests:** whether the tools-only plan converts small owner-operators, and whether the 12-month setup credit is too short for Wix clients locked in for 2+ years.

## Lead 2: Greenway Outdoor Services (Grow)

**Who:** Dana owns a landscaping and hardscaping company in Ridgewood with 12 staff. Their WordPress site was built in 2017, is slow on phones, and still lists 2019 prices.

**How she found us:** a referral from one of our plan clients, who showed her their quote calculator.

**What she says on the call:** "Every night I'm writing estimates for patios and cleanups. Half of them never call back, so I've wasted the time."

**What we recommend:** the Grow plan.

- **New custom site** with basic SEO, replacing the old WordPress site (URLs kept or redirected so she doesn't lose search traffic)
- **Quote calculator:** visitors pick a service, size and materials and get a price range, which filters out people who can't afford her
- **Estimate request with photos** for bigger jobs, so she can quote without a site visit (adds to the plan or replaces the calculator, depending on the edit hours it costs)
- **Headshots add-on:** a team photo and crew shots for the About page, through our photographer

**Cost:** From $3,250 setup (about 5 pages + quote calculator) + $349/month, plus the headshot shoot. Year one, with the add-ons below: about $10,379. Prepaying a year saves her 10–15%, depending on the discount option we pick.

**Spread option:** if $3,250 upfront is what stalls her, she pays $0 setup and about $647/month for the first 12 months ($349 plan + $298 toward setup), then $349. The spread costs about 10% more, so year one comes to about $10,705, and any remaining setup balance is due if she leaves early. She gets one or the other: the spread, or the prepay discount, not both.

**How it plays out:**

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–6 | Kickoff, headshot shoot, design, build, launch |
| Month 3 | Report shows a spike in fall cleanup quotes; she asks about recurring work |
| Month 4 | We add maintenance plan sign-ups (seasonal service subscriptions on Stripe; +$750 setup, +$99/month) |
| Month 9 | Quarterly check-in: few leads from Glen Rock and Wyckoff; we sell ongoing SEO for one location ($325/month) |

**What it tests:** whether the quote calculator is a strong enough hook to sell Grow on its own, and whether referrals from plan clients actually happen, and whether the spread option wins deals that stall on the setup fee.

## Lead 3: Harbor Light Home Care (Operate)

**Who:** Priya runs a home care agency in Hackensack with 25 staff, mostly caregivers. Her website is fine, but the business runs on phone calls, paper intake forms and a scheduling spreadsheet.

**How she found us:** searched for a Hackensack web agency and landed on our city page, then read the 360 Degree Care case study.

**What she says on the call:** "My office manager spends half her day answering families who want to know how Mom is doing. And we're always short on caregivers."

**What we recommend:** the Operate plan, scoped in a paid discovery session first.

- **Rebuilt site** focused on two audiences: families looking for care, and caregivers looking for work
- **Online intake forms** that replace paper and feed straight into her records
- **Caregiver applications** with screening questions, so she only calls qualified applicants
- **Family update portal:** families log in to see visit times and notes instead of calling
- **Owner dashboard:** new inquiries, open applications, active clients in one view

**Cost:** $1,000 paid discovery, then from $6,000 setup (site $2,500, family portal from $2,000, intake forms and caregiver applications $750 each) + from $649/month, with quarterly strategy calls. Year one, billing monthly from month 3: about $13,490.

**Spread option:** not offered for Operate. Discovery is paid upfront, and the setup is paid in milestones as each stage launches.

**Watch out:** intake forms and family notes can include health information, which may bring privacy rules such as HIPAA into play. Before quoting, check what she's required to protect, and whether our hosting and contract can meet it. This is a reason to scope carefully, not to walk away.

**How it plays out:**

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–2 | Paid discovery: map her intake, hiring and family-update process; check privacy requirements |
| Weeks 3–10 | Build in stages: site and applications first, then intake, then the portal |
| Month 6 | Office manager's phone time is down; Priya asks about caregiver scheduling |
| Year 2 | Custom scheduling software, quoted as its own project (our earlier rough estimate for this kind of work: $10–40k) |

**What it tests:** whether Operate can be priced and delivered by two people, how we handle sensitive data, and whether plan clients really lead to custom software projects.

## What these leads tell us

At the new prices, these three clients bring in about $28,285 in year one, setup fees and add-ons included (about $28,611 if Greenway spreads her setup). Walking them through the plan also turns up six gaps to settle:

1. **Setup credit window.** Wix clients can't upgrade for 2+ years, so a 12-month credit never applies to them. Extend it for locked-in clients?
2. **Third-party costs.** Paid Calendly, Cal.com or Stripe fees are the client's. Proposals need to say so and name the level each plan unlocks.
3. **What counts as a tool in Grow.** Is a photo estimate request a second tool, or part of the quote calculator?
4. **Replacing old sites.** Moving a client off an old WordPress site needs redirects so they keep their search traffic. This belongs in onboarding.
5. **Paid discovery for Operate.** Operate is scoped through a paid discovery session, now priced at $1,000 to match our QuickBooks Discovery & Assessment.
6. **Sensitive data.** Care agencies may handle health information. Add it to risks and contracts, and check what our hosting supports before quoting.
