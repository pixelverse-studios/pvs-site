## The offer

In one sentence: "We get local service businesses found on Google, and show you every month what it brought in."

Who it's for: owner-run service businesses in Bergen County with roughly 2–25 staff and a website that's already decent. Businesses whose site is old or broken start with a rebuild.

Why it's attractive: SEO is recurring by nature, clients understand the goal ("show up when people search"), and it builds on what we already do in every build (city pages, schema, Google Business Profile).

Why it's hard: almost every agency and freelancer says the same sentence, so the only thing that sets us apart is proof, and we don't have it yet (see Proof gap).

SEO plans have a 6-month minimum, then run month to month. Results usually take 3–6 months to show, so a shorter term sets clients up to cancel just before it starts working.

### Every SEO plan includes

- A monthly report: calls, form leads, rankings for the client's main searches, Google Business Profile views, and what we did
- Basic site upkeep needed for SEO (speed, broken links, schema)
- Email support with a stated response time
- No ranking guarantees, ever. We promise the work and honest reporting

### Websites in this route

- If the current site is fine, we don't touch it beyond SEO fixes
- If it blocks rankings (very slow, not mobile friendly, no room for city pages), we rebuild at our live package prices: Starter $1,000, Small Business $2,300, Custom Growth $4,350
- Rebuilds are sold with at least 6 months of SEO, so the site never leaves without a plan

### Add-ons

Headshots and team photos, extra pages, ad management, built-in tools (quote calculator, booking) for existing clients only.

## Proof gap

This is the deciding issue for the SEO route. From the evidence gathered on 2026-11-19 (Search Console exports in the repo, client SEO docs, case-study data):

| Where | What the numbers show |
| --- | --- |
| Our own site, last 28 days | 10 clicks, about 1,380 impressions, average position about 23. Every click went to the homepage. 0 organic leads in any recorded month |
| Our city pages | 0 clicks. Positions: Fort Lee 17.8, Englewood 22.1, Paramus 21.5, Hackensack 35.5, Ridgewood 40.8 |
| Best searches | "seo company fort lee" at 5.6 and "web design fort lee nj" at 6.8, each under 10 impressions |
| Our timeline goals | Missed: Fort Lee and Englewood on page 1, Hackensack and Paramus top 10, 2–10 leads a month |
| Jones Pressure Washing | About 10 clicks in 3 months. The 10 pages added in 2026 are known to Google but not crawled yet. 3 backlinks |
| 360 Degree Care | 17 clicks in Nov–Dec 2025, mostly searches for their name; engagement ended |
| Other clients | No performance data collected |
| Google Business Profile | 7 reviews, last one in February |
| Case studies on the site | None claims an SEO number |

What we can honestly show a prospect today: well-built city and service pages on three client sites, thorough audits and reporting, two Fort Lee searches on page 1, and 7 five-star reviews.

What we're missing: one client with a dated before-and-after result (clicks, map-pack rankings or leads), our own site in the top 3 for a search people actually make, and any organic lead.

Also broken today: our Search Console service account has no access to any property, including our own, so automated reporting doesn't work until that's fixed.

## Marketing and sales

Ads for SEO services are crowded and expensive, and they send price shoppers comparing us to cheap packages. In this route our own rankings are the marketing: if we can't rank in Fort Lee, we can't sell SEO in Fort Lee.

- Lead offer: a free local SEO audit (our website review form already exists), shown on the homepage, city pages and blog
- Our city pages and Google Business Profile are the main channel once they rank
- Referrals: a free month for a referral who signs
- Local business groups and chambers: short talks on getting found on Google
- Paid ads: none during the proof sprint; afterwards a small Google Search test on city + "SEO" searches, judged on cost per signed client

### Sales process

- Free audit delivered within 2 business days, as a short video or one-page report
- 20-minute call to walk through it
- Proposal: Foundation + plan, with the 3–6 month timeline stated up front
- Follow up at 3 and 7 days

## Keeping and growing clients

SEO clients cancel when they can't see progress. The monthly report and a quarterly call are what keep them.

| When we see this | We offer this |
| --- | --- |
| Rankings up, but few calls or forms | Website improvements or a rebuild |
| Leads arrive but follow-up is slow | Built-in tools (missed-call text-back, booking), sold as add-ons |
| Steady results in one city | The next plan level, more cities |
| New services or locations | New pages |
| Running the business on spreadsheets | Custom software (the bigger work) |

Warning signs: no reply to reports, questions about "what are we paying for", flat leads at month 4. Call them before month 6, not after.
