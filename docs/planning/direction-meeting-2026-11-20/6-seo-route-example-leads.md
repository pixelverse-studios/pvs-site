## Lead 1: Shine Line Mobile Detailing (Local)

**Who:** Marco runs a mobile car detailing business in Paramus with one helper. He's on a paid Wix plan for 2 more years and likes how his site looks.

**How he found us:** he ran the free SEO audit on our Paramus city page after searching for "car detailing website help."

**What he says on the call:** "I'm under a car all day. By the time I call people back, they've booked someone else."

**What we recommend:** Foundation + Local plan for Paramus.

- Google Business Profile cleanup: categories, service area, photos, weekly posts
- A page for each service (ceramic coating, interior detailing) on his Wix site
- Review requests after every job

**Cost:** $1,500 Foundation + $650/month. Year one: about $9,300.

**Spread option:** $0 upfront and $925/month for the first 6 months ($650 + $275 toward Foundation), then $650. About $9,450 in year one.

**How it plays out:**

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Month 1 | Foundation work; profile and service pages updated |
| Month 3 | Profile views up, a few more calls, but he still misses most of them |
| Month 5 | He asks what he's getting for $650; we pitch missed-call text-back as an add-on |
| Month 6 | Likely decision point: he either adds tools or cancels at the end of the minimum term |

**What it tests:** whether small owner-operators can afford a priced-from-hours SEO plan at all. More traffic doesn't fix his real problem (missed calls), which direction A solved for $198/month.

## Lead 2: Greenway Outdoor Services (Growth)

**Who:** Dana owns a landscaping and hardscaping company in Ridgewood with 12 staff. Their WordPress site was built in 2017, is slow on phones, and still lists 2019 prices.

**How she found us:** a referral from one of our clients.

**What she says on the call:** "We do well in Ridgewood from word of mouth, but nobody in Glen Rock or Wyckoff finds us online."

**What we recommend:** rebuild first, then Foundation + Growth for Ridgewood, Glen Rock and Wyckoff.

- Small Business website package, replacing the old site (URLs kept or redirected so she doesn't lose search traffic)
- City pages for each town, Google Business Profile service areas, review requests
- 2 new pages or posts a month (patios, fall cleanups, seasonal services)

**Cost:** $2,300 website + $1,500 Foundation + $1,150/month. Year one: about $17,600.

**Spread option:** the website spread over 12 months (about $2,530 total, about $211/month extra), Foundation paid upfront. About $17,830 in year one. She gets the spread or the prepay discount, not both.

**How it plays out:**

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Weeks 1–6 | New site built and launched with city pages; Foundation work alongside |
| Month 3 | First impressions in Glen Rock and Wyckoff searches |
| Month 6 | First leads from the new towns; monthly report shows calls and forms by town |
| Month 9 | Quarterly call: she still writes quotes at night; we pitch the quote calculator as an add-on |

**What it tests:** whether a rebuild plus SEO is an easy sell to growing businesses with old sites. This is the SEO route's natural buyer.

## Lead 3: Harbor Light Home Care (Local)

**Who:** Priya runs a home care agency in Hackensack with 25 staff, mostly caregivers. Her website is fine, but the business runs on phone calls, paper intake forms and a scheduling spreadsheet.

**How she found us:** searched for a Hackensack web agency and landed on our city page.

**What she says on the call:** "My office manager spends half her day answering families. And we're always short on caregivers."

**What we recommend:** Foundation + Local, aimed at caregiver recruiting ("caregiver jobs Hackensack") and local care searches.

**Cost:** $1,500 Foundation + $650/month. Year one: about $9,300.

**Spread option:** same as Lead 1: $925/month for 6 months, then $650.

**How it plays out:**

| When | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Month 1 | Foundation work; careers page and profile updated |
| Month 4 | More caregiver applications, all still arriving by phone and email |
| Month 6 | Her office manager is as busy as before; she asks if we "do software" |
| Year 2 | Custom intake and portal work, quoted as its own project, if we still offer it |

**What it tests:** what we lose when the main offer doesn't match the client's problem. Under direction A she was our biggest client in year one. Under the SEO route she's a small one, and her real need goes unsold or to someone else.

## What these leads tell us

At the proposed prices, these three clients bring in about $36,200 in year one, versus about $28,285 under direction A. The SEO route looks bigger on paper because its prices are built from hours, but:

1. **Cancellations change the picture.** If Shine Line and Harbor Light cancel at the 6-month minimum, which is likely since SEO doesn't solve their main problem, year one drops to about $28,400.
2. **Hours are higher.** About 15 hours a month of ongoing SEO work for these three, plus about 30 hours of Foundation work upfront.
3. **At today's prices +15%** ($325 / $625 a month and the live $800 setup), the same three come to about $20,000, with too few hours to produce results.
4. **Only one of the three is a natural fit.** The SEO route works best for growing businesses with a weak online presence (Greenway). Owner-operators and operations-heavy businesses are better served by direction A.
5. **Proof matters more here.** Each of these sales assumes we can point to a result. Today we can't (see Proof gap in the SEO route plan tab).
