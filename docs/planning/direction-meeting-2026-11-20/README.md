## The four tests

Whatever we go all in on has to pass all four:

1. We want to do it for the next 3 years.
2. We're clearly better at it than a local competitor or a template builder.
3. Someone pays every month.
4. A stranger gets it in one line.

## The two worked-up options

| | Direction A: websites that run your business | SEO route: get local businesses found |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One line | "A website that books, quotes and keeps your clients updated, on one monthly plan." | "We get local service businesses found on Google, and show you every month what it brought in." |
| Front door | Website on a monthly plan, or tools added to an existing site | Free local SEO audit, then an SEO retainer |
| Monthly prices | Tools-only $99 per tool; Launch $179; Grow $349; Operate from $649 | Local $650; Growth $1,150; Market $1,750 (or today's +15%: $325 / $625 / $1,000) |
| Setup | Launch from $2,500; Grow from $3,250; Operate $1,000 discovery, then from $6,000 | $1,500 Foundation; rebuilds at live package prices ($1,000 / $2,300 / $4,350) |
| Minimum term | 12 months | 6 months |
| Spread option | Setup over 12 months, about 10% more, 3–4 clients at a time | Foundation at $275/mo extra for 6 months; rebuild over 12 months, about 10% more |
| Proof today | Websites (Jones, 360 Degree Care, Iffer's); tools only in-house (PVS dashboard, Domani) | None documented |
| Biggest risk | Support time growing faster than revenue | We can't show results, so we look like every other agency |
| Before ads | 2–3 plan clients, one tool live for a real client, lead tracking confirmed | 6–9 month proof sprint, then the gate |

Pricing basis: both plans start from current QuickBooks rates ($150/hour, Website Care $49/$69/$99, Local SEO $275/$550/$875). Either way, a client gets the spread or the prepay discount, not both.

## Must-fix in either direction

These come from both site audits. They're on hold in `direction-a-must-fix.md` until a direction is picked.

1. **Testimonials.** Several have no approval or source: Kyle is quoted three different ways, and "2–3 jobs a week" and "bookings more than doubled" are unsourced. Keep one approved quote per client.
2. **Domani.** It's featured as client work, and `llms.txt` calls it a "hair salon". Unfeature it or label it as our own product.
3. **Privacy page.** There's no `/privacy` page.
4. **Reply times.** The site promises "one business day", "1–2 business days" and "same-day" in different places. Pick one.
5. **Ads security setting.** The site's security settings block Google Ads scripts. This only matters if we run ads.

Also worth raising: the public phone number has a 914 (Westchester) area code, and the blog has been stalled since 2026-04-10 with 8 finished posts still unpublished.
