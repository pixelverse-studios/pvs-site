Search visibility and inquiry improvements

November 4, 2026Ready for review

Overview

The planned website updates improve search access, service-area information, inquiry context and SEO/contact paths. They are prepared for review and are not live yet.

Improved

  • More direct contact choices and clearer ongoing SEO fit and progress reporting.
  • SEO packages explain focused website improvements, coordinated local listing work, and broader content and inquiry-path work. Every plan includes foundational research, with pricing unchanged.
  • Service-area pages connect visitors to relevant services and regional work.
  • A quieter SEO price summary pairs the monthly starting rate with the one-time setup fee, with clear typography on desktop and mobile.
  • Town-page explanations focus on the services each business actually offers.

Fixed

  • Public rendering resources are no longer broadly excluded from crawling.
  • Service towns describe coverage rather than separate offices.
  • Inquiry context retains limited landing and referral information when available.
  • The website uses the confirmed public business phone and explains client-location or agreed-location meetings.
  • Contact information now shares only the public base town and service area, with no private-address map.
  • Review ratings appear only when matching business data is available; unavailable ratings do not become five-star claims.
  • Acquisition context handles invalid campaign codes and later external visits more accurately.
  • Required inquiry selectors have clear labels, and the footer review action remains readable on mobile.

Removed or replaced

  • Town-count package names, undefined page-update quotas and automatic article/posting quotas have been replaced by research-informed scopes.
  • The precise private-address map was removed from the footer.

What happens next

  • The updates will become available after an approved release.
